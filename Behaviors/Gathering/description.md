# Gathering

> Clustering of people in a relatively stationary group position (the group's central point doesn't move much). Clustering: people acting at a similar speed (0 for stationary activities) and staying close to each other for a period of time. Socialization layer: clustering within 2.1 m, with body or head orientation toward each other at some point during the clustering.
